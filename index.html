How to play ☁️

Keep bouncing and climb as high as you can.

  • Move: tap and hold anywhere on the playfield, then drag left or right to steer. On computers, click and drag, or use ← → / A / D.
  • Land: touch a platform from above to bounce automatically.
  • Stars: collect them for bonus points.
  • Springs: launch much higher!
  • Wrap around: leave one side and reappear on the other.

Scores are saved on this device. Share your result with friends to compare runs. A live cross-device leaderboard would need an online server, so this version stays fully offline.
