How to play ☁️

Keep bouncing and climb as high as you can.

  • Move: tap and hold anywhere on the playfield, then drag left or right to steer. On computers, click and drag, or use ← → / A / D.
  • Land: touch a platform from above to bounce automatically.
  • Stars: collect them for bonus points.
  • Springs: launch much higher!
  • Power-ups: collect jetpacks for a burst upward.
  • Hazards: avoid floating storm-bots.
  • Coins: collect gold coins to unlock character colors.
  • Sky zones: climb 500 m to discover a new color world.
  • Wrap around: leave one side and reappear on the other.

Scores are saved on this device. Share your result with friends to compare runs. This build keeps scores on this device. To create a true shared leaderboard for all friends, we still need to connect an online score service.
